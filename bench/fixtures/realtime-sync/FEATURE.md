# Team notes board

- The home page `/` is a shared notes board. Type a note into `#note` (placeholder "Write a note") and press the "Post" button `#post-btn`.
- Posted notes appear as yellow cards inside `#board`.
- The board refreshes itself about twice a second, so notes posted by anyone on the team show up without reloading.
- Notes are shown in the order they were posted.
- Blank notes are ignored.
